# Pulse Strategy

## Target problem

Founders, managers, and small teams do real work through several AI harnesses, sessions, and projects, but the context that makes their work good stays fragmented. They either repeat themselves, accept low-quality improvised memory, or risk leaking private and client context through an undifferentiated global memory pool.

## Our approach

Win with project-first memory whose scope is enforced before retrieval: Personal makes same-person continuity effortless and local, while Team adds a central governed Vault and Board for shared context. Keep installation simple for an individual and charge where coordination, isolation, review, and administration become a team problem.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** AI-native small-team leaders - They're hiring Pulse to make decisions, conclusions, and proven ways of working available to every authorized person and agent without mixing projects or exposing private context.

**Secondary:** Individual founders and managers - They're hiring Pulse Personal to preserve useful project context across sessions and harnesses while reducing repeated context uploads and prompt tokens.

## Key metrics

- **Personal activation rate** - Share of completed installs that produce one stored memory and one receipt-backed offer to a fresh task inside the same project; measured from local install and continuity receipts.
- **Context economy** - Comparable avoided input tokens and reduction percentage per active project, labeled measured, estimated, or collecting; measured from delivery and host-observation receipts.
- **Project isolation** - Cross-project or unauthorized cross-member influence incidents in retrieval, traces, counts, graph, and continuity; measured by regression gates and dogfood incidents, with a target of zero.
- **Team memory loop completion** - Weekly team projects where at least two members complete candidate, exact approval, shared retrieval, and acknowledgement; measured in Team Vault lifecycle and delivery receipts.
- **Shared-memory usefulness** - Share of approved Team memories that are later retrieved, acknowledged, corrected, or deliberately superseded rather than remaining unused; measured in the Team Vault lifecycle.

## Tracks

### Personal wedge

Make Pulse installable in one command through Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex and prove same-project continuity plus honest token economy without team infrastructure.

_Why it serves the approach:_ A person experiences the value before Pulse asks them to adopt a shared service or change how their team works.

### Project scope and retrieval core

Make trusted project binding, Unassigned Inbox behavior, pre-retrieval visibility, provenance, and state-aware ranking one consistent contract across every harness and storage mode.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Project isolation is the trust mechanism that makes persistent memory useful instead of dangerous or noisy.

### Team Vault and Board

Turn the existing Team security foundation into one real two-person product flow with central projects, roles, exact approval, lifecycle, correction, revocation, deletion, and visible receipts.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Teams pay for governed coordination and one authoritative place to understand what their people and agents remember.

## Not working on

- Global cross-project retrieval or automatic scope broadening.
- Raw transcript, unrestricted document, or credential storage inside Pulse memory.
- Broad connector expansion before the two-person Team nucleus works end to end.
- A skill marketplace or general workflow factory built into Pulse.
- Enterprise billing, compliance claims, or multi-region infrastructure before the core Team loop is proven.

## Marketing

**One-liner:** Project memory that follows your work, not your data leaks.

**Key message:** Personal Pulse remembers the right context across sessions and harnesses. Pulse Team gives the whole team one governed place to approve, inspect, correct, and safely reuse what its people and agents learn.
